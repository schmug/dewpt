Somewhere in this air there is a temperature at which the invisible gives up and becomes a droplet. This is a walk to that temperature.

dewpt is an ambient ideation canvas. You give it a seed — a phrase you care about — and words begin to condense out of latent space: ideas near and far, appearing and disappearing like weather.

stn 02 — condensation

The field never sits still.

Words fade in at random, live five to ten seconds, and evaporate. Near words render sharp and bright; far ones sit faint in the fog — depth is drawn with light, not lines.

And nothing here ever waits on the machine. A deep pool of scored candidates is always ready behind the field, so the weather can't stall — the pop-in must feel like weather, not like loading.

stn 03 — prospecting

Click where nothing is.

Blank space isn't empty — it's unsaturated. Prospect it: a pulse rolls out from your cursor, and a handful of words precipitates right there, drawn a little stranger than the ambient weather.

The gaps are the instrument. The best ideas live where you haven't looked yet.

stn 04 — instruments

Three dials shape the weather.

dewpoint is strangeness — how far from the seed the air is allowed to let go. Low, and only the obvious condenses; high, and the far-field precipitates: pale slate, then lilac, then warm ember.

altitude is abstraction — concrete tactics below, principles above. drizzle is only the spawn rate; it changes how often, never what.

Watch the field as you pass — the dewpoint is rising.

stn 05 — condensate

Pin what catches you.
The rest evaporates.

Everything unpinned returns to vapor. A small sidebar of ghosts — the last twenty evaporated — is the only mercy.
